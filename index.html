The Gentlemen Philosophy

A Return to the Golden Age of True Men's Grooming

We believe that a haircut is not merely routine maintenance—it is an architectural ritual that transforms how a man carries himself in the world.

Established in Zamalek, THE GENTLEMEN was founded to revive the lost art of classical English and Continental barbering. Step into our vintage brass and dark espresso lounge, enjoy a freshly pulled double espresso or single malt, and entrust your hair and beard to master craftsmen who measure their work in millimeters.
